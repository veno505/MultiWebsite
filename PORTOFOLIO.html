CommerceHub

WebGL E-Commerce

The Challenge

Creating a highly performant 3D try-on experience for premium techwear directly in the browser. The requirement was flawless rendering of complex fabric textures without compromising frame rates on mobile devices.

The Solution

Implemented a custom WebGL renderer utilizing optimized GLTF models. Wrote custom GLSL shaders to handle dynamic lighting and fabric material simulation, achieving a steady 60FPS across all modern devices.
